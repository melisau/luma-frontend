A few things you
might be wondering.

Can I make the invitation match my event?

Yes. Personalize the colors, photos, and details for your celebration. Every event can have its own look.

Do my guests need an account?

No. Your guests can open your invitation from a shared link, RSVP, and share photos and messages without creating an account.

Where do photos and messages go?

They stay together in your event’s gallery and guestbook. You can view and manage them from your organizer dashboard.
